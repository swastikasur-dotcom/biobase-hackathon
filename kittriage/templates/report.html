What Tamarind can predict

{{admet}}

The live ADMET tool accepts SMILES. These output definitions come from ADMET-AI model metadata; confirm actual returned columns at runtime. Half-life and volume-of-distribution endpoints have weak validation in that metadata and should not drive a confident hard decision. No Tamarind job or result is implied by listing an endpoint.

VEGFR2 is the anti-target flag for this target.

The VEGFR-active multikinase inhibitors used in GIST carry hypertension and hand-foot skin reaction as characteristic adverse effects. That motivates a selectivity flag, while causal attribution to VEGFR2 for any particular KIT inhibitor remains unestablished, so this is weighted rather than a hard gate. The provisional 30-fold margin is design policy. Calculate it only from comparable IC50 values; engagement probability is not potency. S20
