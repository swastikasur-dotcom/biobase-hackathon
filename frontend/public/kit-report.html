S1

Complementary activity of tyrosine kinase inhibitors against secondary kit mutations in imatinib-resistant gastrointestinal stromal tumours. ↗

Patient-derived GIST models show complementary inhibitor activity across secondary KIT mutations. V654A, T670I and activation-loop variants do not share one sensitivity profile. This supports genotype-resolved screening rather than WT-only ranking.

2019-02-22 · preclinical · Paperclip document metadata; source findings curated against primary publication
S2

Avapritinib Versus Regorafenib in Locally Advanced Unresectable or Metastatic GI Stromal Tumor: A Randomized, Open-Label Phase III Study. ↗

VOYAGER did not demonstrate superior PFS for avapritinib over regorafenib in molecularly unselected third-line or later GIST. This finding is population-specific and does not negate activity in PDGFRA D842V disease.

2021-08-03 · clinical_paper · Paperclip document metadata; source findings curated against primary publication
S3

Ripretinib Versus Sunitinib in Patients With Advanced Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumor After Treatment With Imatinib (INTRIGUE): A Randomized, Open-Label, Phase III Trial. ↗

INTRIGUE did not demonstrate superior PFS for second-line ripretinib over sunitinib. Activity and tolerability findings remain relevant; a missed superiority endpoint is not proof of no activity.

2022-08-10 · clinical_paper · Paperclip document metadata; source findings curated against primary publication
S4

Ripretinib versus sunitinib in gastrointestinal stromal tumor: ctDNA biomarker analysis of the phase 3 INTRIGUE trial. ↗

Exploratory INTRIGUE ctDNA analyses suggest different relative treatment effects in KIT exon 11 plus 13/14 versus exon 11 plus 17/18 populations. These subgroup results are hypothesis-generating, not a replacement for a prospective genotype-specific trial.

2024-01-05 · clinical_paper · Paperclip document metadata; source findings curated against primary publication
S5

Heterogeneity of kinase inhibitor resistance mechanisms in GIST. ↗

Different metastases and subclones can harbor different secondary resistance mutations; KIT loss and amplification were also observed. A single mutation model cannot represent every escape mechanism.

2008-09-01 · preclinical · Europe PMC metadata; analyst-reviewed primary publication
S6

Phase I dose escalation study of NMS-1286937, an orally available Polo-Like Kinase 1 inhibitor, in patients with advanced or metastatic solid tumors. ↗

A phase I study of the PLK1 inhibitor NMS-1286937 reported thrombocytopenia and neutropenia as major dose-limiting toxicities. This motivates PLK1 as an anti-target to assess; it does not establish PLK1 as the cause of toxicity for KIT inhibitors.

2017-07-20 · clinical_paper · Europe PMC metadata; analyst-reviewed primary publication
S7

Experimental and computational approaches to estimate solubility and permeability in drug discovery and development settings. ↗

Physicochemical heuristics identify oral absorption and permeation risk. Treat molecular weight, lipophilicity and hydrogen-bond counts as triage flags rather than universal exclusion rules for kinase inhibitors.

2001-03-01 · preclinical · Europe PMC metadata; analyst-reviewed primary publication
S8

Association of Combination of Conformation-Specific KIT Inhibitors With Clinical Benefit in Patients With Refractory Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors: A Phase 1b/2a Nonrandomized Clinical Trial. ↗

A small nonrandomized phase 1b/2a study investigated conformation-complementary KIT inhibitors PLX9486 and sunitinib in refractory GIST. Combination activity is an experimental clinical observation, not evidence that arbitrary co-dependent targets make effective combinations.

2021-09-01 · clinical_paper · Europe PMC metadata; analyst-reviewed primary publication
S9

ADMET-AI: a machine learning ADMET platform for evaluation of large-scale chemical libraries ↗

ADMET-AI provides learned ADMET endpoints and computed physicochemical descriptors. Half-life and distribution predictions require particular caution; endpoint predictions do not establish a dose-to-tumor-response PK/PD model.

2024-06-19 · preclinical · Primary methods paper and current model endpoint metadata
S10

Ptarmigan-1 API reference ↗

Only catalog human proteins may be screened through the current API. Engagement probability is a prediction, not IC50; fixed probability cutoffs do not establish binding. Reference proteome off-target predictions are web-app only.

Publication date not assigned · tool_documentation · Official OpenAPI and agent documentation
S11

DepMap co-dependency scoring ↗

DepMap co-dependency is based on Pearson correlation of dependency profiles. Correlation alone does not establish synthetic lethality, resistance causality or clinical combination efficacy.

2022-05-06 · data_documentation · Official DepMap community explanation
S12

Proteome Phenome Atlas ↗

Plasma protein associations are distinct from tumor somatic genotype and target-inhibition effects. KIT assay membership must be checked in an identified complete panel table before reporting presence or absence.
